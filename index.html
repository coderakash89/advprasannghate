FAQ

Common Questions About Property Documents in Pune

Which property documents can be drafted and registered in Wagholi?

Sale deeds, agreement to sale, gift deeds, release deeds, wills, power of attorney, rent and leave-and-licence agreements, and other property documents under Maharashtra registration law — for clients in Wagholi, Kharadi and across Pune.

Can an NRI get property documents prepared and registered in Pune from abroad?

Yes. We regularly assist NRI clients, including drafting a power of attorney for registration, so documents can be executed and registered without you needing to travel.

How much stamp duty and registration fee applies to a sale deed?

It depends on the property value, document type and location within Maharashtra. We calculate the exact stamp duty and registration fee for your document and handle payment and registration for you.

Do I need to visit the office in person?

For most documents an initial discussion can happen over call or WhatsApp. Registration requires presence as per law — but for NRI and outstation clients we arrange this through a power of attorney where permitted.
